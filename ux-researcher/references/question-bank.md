# Question bank (FR / EN)

Questions organized by what they are for. Two rules govern everything here: ask about past behavior and concrete incidents, not hypotheticals or opinions; and keep the question neutral so it does not contain its own answer. For neutrality patterns, see `bias-audit.md`.

## Interview questions

### Question types (use the right tool)

- **Grand tour:** open the topic widely, let them lead. "Walk me through a typical day when you..." / "Raconte-moi une journee typique ou tu..."
- **Specific incident (critical incident):** anchor to a real, recent event. Stronger than generalities. "Tell me about the last time you..." / "Parle-moi de la derniere fois ou tu..."
- **Process / behavior:** trace what they actually did. "What did you do right before that?" / "Qu'est-ce que tu as fait juste avant?"
- **Contrast:** reveal criteria through comparison. "How is that different from when you..." / "En quoi c'est different de quand tu..."
- **Difficulty / workaround:** surface unmet needs. "What was the most frustrating part?" / "Quel a ete le moment le plus frustrant?" and "Did you find a way around it?" / "As-tu trouve un contournement?"
- **Magic wand (use sparingly, late):** "If you could change one thing about that, what would it be?" / "Si tu pouvais changer une seule chose, ce serait quoi?" Treat answers as signals of pain, not as feature requests.

### Probe / follow-up bank

The interview is won in the follow-ups. Keep these ready:

- Echo: repeat their last words back as a question. "Confusing?" / "Confus?"
- Silence: wait. People fill it with the real answer.
- Expand: "Say more about that." / "Dis-m'en plus."
- Define: "What do you mean by [their word]?" / "Qu'est-ce que tu entends par [leur mot]?"
- Concretize: "Can you walk me through exactly how that went?" / "Tu peux me montrer exactement comment ca s'est passe?"
- Why-behind: "What made you do it that way?" / "Qu'est-ce qui t'a amene a faire comme ca?"

### By research goal

**Discovery / generative**
- "Walk me through how you currently handle [task]." / "Montre-moi comment tu gères [tache] en ce moment."
- "What's the hardest part of [activity] for you?" / "C'est quoi le plus difficile dans [activite]?"
- "Last time you did [task], what got in your way?" / "La derniere fois que tu as fait [tache], qu'est-ce qui t'a bloque?"

**Problem validation**
- "Tell me about the last time [problem] happened." / "Parle-moi de la derniere fois ou [probleme] est arrive."
- "How often does that come up?" / "Ca arrive a quelle frequence?"
- "What did it cost you when it happened, time, money, stress?" / "Ca t'a coute quoi quand c'est arrive, temps, argent, stress?"
- "What do you do today to deal with it?" / "Tu fais quoi aujourd'hui pour gerer ca?"

**Prioritization (what matters most)**
- "Of everything we talked about, what would you fix first?" / "De tout ce qu'on a vu, tu reglerais quoi en premier?"
- "Walk me through how you chose between [option A] and [option B]." / "Explique-moi comment tu as choisi entre [A] et [B]."

**Usability (with a prototype, think-aloud)**
- "Tell me what you're trying to do here." / "Dis-moi ce que tu essaies de faire ici."
- "What did you expect to happen when you clicked that?" / "Tu t'attendais a quoi en cliquant la?"
- "What would you do next?" (never "is this clear?") / "Tu ferais quoi ensuite?"

**Satisfaction / experience (retrospective)**
- "Think back to [experience]. What stood out?" / "Repense a [experience]. Qu'est-ce qui t'a marque?"
- "Was there a moment you almost gave up?" / "Y a-t-il eu un moment ou tu as failli abandonner?"

## Survey questions

Surveys measure prevalence. They cannot probe, so wording must be self-contained and unambiguous.

### Patterns

- **Rating scale:** default to a 5-point scale, labeled at every point, balanced around a neutral midpoint. Avoid binary yes/no when a degree exists.
  - EN: Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neutral / Agree / Strongly agree
  - FR: Fortement en desaccord / En desaccord / Neutre / D'accord / Fortement d'accord
- **Behavioral frequency:** use concrete anchors, not "often/rarely". "In the past 7 days, how many times did you...?" / "Dans les 7 derniers jours, combien de fois as-tu...?"
- **Single vs multi-select:** single-select forces a priority; multi-select measures coverage. Choose deliberately. Add "Other" with a text field and "None of these".
- **Open text:** one or two max, placed last. "What is the one thing you would change about [X]?" / "Quelle est la seule chose que tu changerais a propos de [X]?"

### Survey hygiene

- One idea per question (no double-barreled). Split "easy and useful" into two questions.
- Define any term that could be read two ways.
- Order: easy and concrete first, sensitive or demographic last.
- Always offer a non-answer ("Prefer not to say" / "Je prefere ne pas repondre") on sensitive items.
- Pilot with 3 to 5 people before sending; watch where they hesitate.

## Output format for this mode

Group questions by goal or by interview phase. For each, give the EN and FR versions. Mark any question that risks bias and point to its fix. Keep a set to roughly 8 to 12 core questions for an interview; more than that will not fit a 45 to 60 minute session with room to probe.
